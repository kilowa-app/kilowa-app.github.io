Kilowa: the EV charging log for iPhone

What does your electric car really cost? Kilowa works it out from your own charges: per 100 km, base fees included. Free, no account, your data stays with you.

{% include appstore.html %}Support

For iPhone with iOS 26 or later and iPhone Duo · English and German · kilometers or miles

What Kilowa works out for you

Log a charge with kWh or the total and your odometer reading. Kilowa does the rest: price per kWh, consumption, cost per 100 km and where your money goes. At your home wallbox or a public charger, on a tariff or ad hoc.

Real cost per 100 km

Base fees for your wallbox, charging card or plan are included pro rata. You see what a kilometer really costs, not just the electricity.

Log in seconds

Siri, Shortcuts, Control Center and the Action button open a prefilled entry. With Pro, Siri logs a charge without opening the app.

Tariffs like in real life

Providers with several prices, such as peak and off-peak at your wallbox. Charging abroad with foreign currency and your own exchange rate.

Your data stays with you

No account, no ads, no tracking, no server of its own. Everything stays on your iPhone and, optionally, in your private iCloud.

How it works

  1. Add your car and tariff. A short tour guides you through your first tariff.
  2. Log a charge. Enter kWh or the amount and your odometer reading. Done.
  3. See the results. The dashboard shows cost per 100 km, price per kWh, consumption and a comparison with a petrol car based on your assumptions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Kilowa cost?

Kilowa is free with unlimited charges. Kilowa Pro is an optional one-time purchase that adds features such as logging with Siri without opening the app, widgets, a tariff check, an annual PDF report and multiple vehicles. Pro Family is planned for version 1.1: Pro for up to six people in an Apple family group.

Do I need an account?

No. Kilowa works without an account or sign-in. There is no server of its own.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and, if you like, in your private iCloud. Kilowa shows no ads and has no tracking. The only connection outside Apple fetches exchange rates from the European Central Bank. See the privacy policy.

Why does Kilowa show higher consumption than my car?

Kilowa uses the billed kWh, the electricity you pay for, which includes charging losses. The car usually shows only what leaves the battery. That is why Kilowa matches your bill and sits a little above the car's display.

How does Kilowa calculate cost per 100 km?

Charging costs plus the share of base fees in the period, divided by the distance driven, times 100. The distance comes from the odometer readings you enter with each charge. See an example with the calculator.

Which devices does Kilowa run on?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, in English and German, with kilometers or miles. On the unfolded iPhone Duo, Kilowa uses the large screen in two halves. There is currently no Android version.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Kilowa exports your charges as a CSV file that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

About Kilowa

Kilowa is made in Zurich. Joerg Wesiak built the app because he kept the charging costs of his own electric car in a spreadsheet and wanted something simpler on his iPhone.
