Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

Jörg Wesiak, Ueberlandstrasse 5, 8050 Zurich, Switzerland
Email: kilowa.app@gmail.com

This policy follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Principle

Kilowa does not require an account. The Provider runs no servers for the App and receives none of the data you enter in the App. The App contains no third-party software for analytics, crash reporting or advertising. There is no tracking and no sale or other sharing of your data with third parties. The only connection outside Apple is the retrieval of exchange rates (section 6).

3. Data in the App

The App stores your input locally on your iPhone: vehicles with an optional photo, providers and tariffs, charging locations, charging sessions with notes and recurring costs, plus your settings. For the widget, the App keeps a summary of your key figures in a protected area on your iPhone.

Charging sessions may appear in iPhone search (Spotlight). This search index stays on your device. Some features use Apple Intelligence directly on the device. No data leaves your iPhone in the process.

4. iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled for Kilowa, your data is stored in your private iCloud storage and synced between your devices. This data sits in your personal iCloud account. The Provider has no access to it. Apple processes it under its own privacy policy. You can switch off iCloud for Kilowa at any time in the iOS settings.

5. Permissions

  • Photos: For the vehicle photo, the App uses the iOS photo picker. The App receives only the image you select and has no access to your photo library. When saving, the App removes metadata such as the capture location (GPS) and camera details.
  • Camera: The App opens the camera only when you choose “Take photo”. The photo is stored only in the App.
  • Siri and Shortcuts (Pro): Apple processes voice commands under its own privacy policy. The App only receives the values needed to record an entry.
  • The App does not use your location, does not send notifications and does not access your contacts.

6. Exchange rates

To convert costs between currencies, the App retrieves exchange rates from the European Central Bank (ECB) in Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Only currency codes and a date range are sent, no data from the App. As with any request over the internet, the ECB sees your IP address. You can switch off automatic retrieval in the App under “Fetch rates automatically”.

7. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Provider receives no payment data and no personal details, only aggregated sales statistics from Apple.

8. Statistics and crash reports from Apple

If you agree in the iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides the Provider with anonymised usage statistics and crash reports. The Provider cannot identify individuals from them.

9. Export

If you export data from the App, you decide where the file goes. From then on, the terms of the chosen recipient or service apply.

10. Contact by email

If you email the Provider, your address and message are used only to handle your request. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail), which may involve a transfer of data to the USA. Messages are deleted once they are no longer needed to handle the request.

11. Website

The Kilowa website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., USA. When you visit the website, GitHub records technical data such as your IP address and stores it for security purposes. This may involve a transfer of data to the USA. The Provider has no access to this data; GitHub’s privacy statement applies. The website uses no cookies, no tracking and loads no content from other third parties.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data. Since your App data is stored only on your device and in your iCloud account, you can delete it yourself at any time by deleting the App and the Kilowa data in your iCloud settings. For requests, write to kilowa.app@gmail.com.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Switzerland with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

13. Changes

The Provider will update this policy before introducing new features that use additional data. The current version applies.
