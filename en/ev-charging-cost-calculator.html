EV Charging Cost Calculator

What does your electric car cost per 100 km once the base fee for your wallbox, charging card or plan is included? Enter your numbers. The fields are prefilled with example values, not current market prices.

All amounts in your currency, for example EUR or CHF. The calculator runs only in your browser and stores nothing.

How it is calculated

Electricity per 100 km = consumption × electricity price
Base fee per 100 km = base fees per month ÷ km per month × 100
Cost per 100 km = electricity per 100 km + base fee per 100 km

Example with the prefilled values: 18 kWh × 0.30 = 5.40 for electricity. A base fee of 20 at 1,000 km a month adds 2.00 per 100 km. Together that is 7.40 per 100 km. The example petrol car uses 6.5 liters at 1.80, so 11.70 per 100 km.

The base fee matters most if you drive little: at 500 km a month it would be 4.00 instead of 2.00 per 100 km in the example.

Which consumption figure should I use?

Your car usually shows what leaves the battery. Charging losses come on top, and you pay for them too. For the most accurate figure, use the kWh billed on your electricity bill or at the charger: charged kWh ÷ km driven × 100.
